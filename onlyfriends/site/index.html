OnlyFriends

Instagram, just your people.

A Firefox extension that keeps messages, filters for selected friends when the page allows it, and quietly puts Explore and Reels out of easy reach.

Get the package Open Instagram

Install on Firefox Desktop

  1. Download the unsigned build below.
  2. Unzip, or use the built dist/ from a local clone.
  3. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  4. Load Temporary Add-on → choose manifest.json.
  5. Visit Instagram signed in → tap the on-page OF button.

Temporary add-ons disappear when Firefox restarts. Stable Firefox Android needs a Mozilla-signed XPI — an unsigned ZIP is not installable there.

What this site is

This Railway service is a static landing + artifact host. It does not talk to Instagram, does not collect credentials, and does not store your friends list. Extension preferences stay in your browser.

  • Strict Friends filtering depends on readable usernames in Instagram’s DOM.
  • Story viewer swipe chains are not safely filterable.
  • Ad hiding is best-effort, never complete.

Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta. Private MVP hosting.
